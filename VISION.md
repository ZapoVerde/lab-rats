# Vision

Build a polished browser-based kinetic simulation centered on balls multiplying through collisions.

The experience should be immediately understandable: a large circular arena contains moving balls. The balls move continuously, bounce naturally from the arena boundary and from one another, and every collision between two balls creates another ball.

The simulation is deliberately simple, but the finished product should not feel like a technical demo. It should feel intentionally designed, visually compelling, responsive, and exceptionally smooth.

As the number of balls grows, the experience should remain coherent and usable rather than rapidly degrading into obvious instability or poor performance.

The implementation should stay understandable and appropriately small. Prefer clear engineering and deliberate design over unnecessary complexity.
